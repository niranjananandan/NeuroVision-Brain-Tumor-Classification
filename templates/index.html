NEUROVISION • SYSTEM

About System

Understanding the NeuroVision AI-based MRI classification system.

NeuroVision Brain MRI Classification

NeuroVision is a deep learning based brain MRI image classification project. The system accepts an MRI image from the user, preprocesses the image, and passes it through a trained neural network model.

Processing Pipeline

The uploaded MRI image is converted to grayscale, resized to 128 × 128 pixels, normalized between 0 and 1, and provided to the trained model for classification.

Classification Output

The model produces a prediction score which is converted into either TUMOR or NORMAL classification along with a confidence percentage.

BRAIN MRI • INFORMATION

About Brain Tumor

General educational information about brain tumors and MRI-based analysis.

Normal Brain

A normal brain MRI generally shows the expected anatomical structures without an abnormal mass or lesion identified by the imaging study.

Brain Tumor

A brain tumor is an abnormal growth of cells within or around the brain. MRI is commonly used to visualize brain structures and identify abnormalities that may require further clinical evaluation.

Possible Effects

Depending on its location and characteristics, a brain tumor may be associated with symptoms such as headaches, seizures, vision changes, weakness, or other neurological changes.

AI Classification

NeuroVision provides an AI-based classification of the uploaded MRI image. The result is intended for educational and research purposes and should not be used as a medical diagnosis.
