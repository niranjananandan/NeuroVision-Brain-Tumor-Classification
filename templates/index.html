Understanding Brain Tumors

Visual reference and general information about normal and tumor-affected brain MRI.

🧠 Normal Brain MRI

Typical appearance of brain structures without an identified tumor lesion.

⚠️ Tumor-Affected MRI

MRI may show an abnormal mass or lesion depending on tumor type, location and imaging characteristics.

What is a Brain Tumor?

A brain tumor is an abnormal growth of cells in or around the brain. Brain tumors can differ in their type, size, location and growth behavior.

Possible Effects

Depending on its location and characteristics, a brain tumor may affect nearby brain tissue and can be associated with different neurological symptoms.

Headache and nausea
Seizures
Vision or hearing changes
Balance or movement difficulties
Speech, memory or concentration changes

Possible Causes & Risk Factors

For many primary brain tumors, the exact cause is not known. Certain genetic changes and, in some cases, significant exposure to ionizing radiation can be associated with increased risk.

Why MRI Analysis?

MRI provides detailed images of brain structures. AI-based image classification can assist research by identifying patterns within MRI images.

Important: The visual examples and information in this section are provided for educational purposes. MRI appearance can vary widely, and only qualified medical professionals can interpret clinical imaging and provide a diagnosis.

What is NeuroVision?

NeuroVision is a deep learning based brain MRI image classification project. It provides an interface where users can upload an MRI image and obtain an AI-generated classification result.
